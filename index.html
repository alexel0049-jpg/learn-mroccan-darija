What is Darija?

Darija is the everyday spoken Arabic of Morocco, different from Modern Standard Arabic (Fusha), and different from Egyptian or Levantine Arabic too. It's shaped by Amazigh (Berber), French, and Spanish influence, and it's what you'll actually hear in the street, in cafés, and at home. This site focuses on real, spoken Darija, the way people genuinely talk.
